## New releases and listening links

- [Folk Knock — Lights of Prague](https://folkknock.bandcamp.com/album/lights-of-prague): **Noctivagate** (hanter dro), **Dina and her musical house / Thanks, Pien!** (schottishes), **Šapito perdu** (mazurka). Also **Mazurka de la rentrée** and **Scottish du cirque en friche / City streets / Lights of Prague**. These are strong catalogue-based candidates, not newly measured timing maps.
- Folk Knock's **Leipzig autumn waltz** is labelled **mazurka**. **The best is yet to come** is a mazurka in five; **The tram waltz / The snowy spruce** are waltzes in five. Exclude those five-count pieces from the current ordinary mazurka/waltz presets.
- [ba.fnu feat. Beat Bouet Trio — Jsem já synek](https://bafnu.bandcamp.com/track/jsem-j-synek-krajcipolka-chapelloise): chapelloise candidate from a preferred band.
- [ba.fnu — 10 . refused . reduced](https://bafnu.bandcamp.com/album/10-refused-reduced): **cirque cirkusy jen** (cercle), **valse de jana** (valse), **l'orrient express** (scottish). **ej lásko** is labelled mazurka-valse, so needs section handling.
- [ba.fnu — skočíš](https://bafnu.bandcamp.com/track/sko-scottish): another explicitly labelled schottische. [Zabili](https://bafnu.bandcamp.com/track/zabili-mazurka) remains the mazurka priority.
- [Naragonia — Alio, Tandem](https://www.naragonia.com/shop/products/cd-tandem?variant=41847353671746); [Boom bal version](https://homerecordsbe.bandcamp.com/track/alio). Exact version matters.
- [Naragonia — 20th anniversary concert](https://naragonia.bandcamp.com/album/20th-anniversary-concert): public listening source for live **Calimero** and **Hellebore / Too Late to Sleep**. [Composer's Calimero workshop notes](https://www.tanzvolk-leipzig.de/session-tunes/) identify the original on *Janneke Tarzan* as hanter-dro.
- [Bargainatt — Branle des chevaliers](https://bargainatt.bandcamp.com/track/branle-des-chevaliers): Noirmoutier candidate, previously represented by a database-labelled preview in the analysis. [C'est pas banane !](https://bargainatt.bandcamp.com/album/cest-pas-banane) also contains **Captain Scottish**, **Banana Circle** and **Elna**; further listening candidates, not mapped presets.
- [Trio Loubelya — Mazurka du Dimanche](https://trioloubelya.bandcamp.com/track/mazurka-du-dimanche): a concrete Lovembal-connected addition. Do not substitute tracks from their asymmetrical-waltz/mazurka compilation indiscriminately.
- [Feather and Fox — A Winter Morning](https://featherandfox.bandcamp.com/): **Hinterhof Mazurka** is the first candidate to check for the existing dances. Instrumentation is cello and mandolin; a different ensemble texture from the other choices. Full arrangement remains unchecked.
- [Żniwa — Dwa Żywioły](https://zniwaband.bandcamp.com/album/dwa-ywio-y): **Drumul Draculi** and **Burning Andro**. Their [Les Danses](https://zniwaband.bandcamp.com/album/les-danses) is a historical-dance side project; titles such as **Scotch Cap** are not evidence of ordinary schottische compatibility.
- [Jonathan Gabay — Tzadik Katamar](https://musicfromisrael.bandcamp.com/track/tzadik-katamar); [Effi Netzer and his band — Tzadik Katamar](https://music-from-israel.bandcamp.com/track/tzadik-katamar).

## Suggested selection order

Use the familiar local tracks first: **Jopelloise, Starwars, Lora Luna, Jottish, Café Coiffeur**, with **Allez, bourrés** and **YFK Hanter dro**. Keep **Zabili / Alio** as the mazurka pair and **Żniwa's Drumul**. Add **Bargainatt's Branle des chevaliers** for Noirmoutier and the named **Tzadik Katamar** reference.

Then broaden the comparison set with Folk Knock's **Noctivagate / Dina… / Šapito perdu**, Naragonia's **Calimero / Hellebore…**, ba.fnu's **Jsem já synek**, Trio Loubelya's **Mazurka du Dimanche**, and Feather and Fox's **Hinterhof Mazurka**. Before any new app preset, choose the exact recording and validate its count rate, first figure, section boundaries and ending.
